How it works
From audit to insight in four steps

Every engagement starts free. We earn your trust before we earn your business.

Step 01

Free territorial audit

We analyse your current footprint and identify your top 3 untapped opportunities using real ABS and competitor data. Delivered in 5 days, no commitment.

Step 02

Data readiness check

We review your customer and sales data. Most businesses have address inconsistencies, duplicates or gaps — we scope what needs fixing before analysis starts.

Step 03

Analysis & delivery

We run the geomarketing analysis using ABS Census, GNAF, competitor data and your cleaned records. Delivered as an interactive map or PDF report.

Step 04

Ongoing intelligence

Optional retainer: a live location dashboard that updates as your business and market evolves. Add AI automation to handle reporting automatically.
