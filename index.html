How it works

Every engagement starts small. The $299 Territory Audit is fully credited against your project — we earn your trust before we earn your business.

Step 02

Data readiness check

If you have existing customer or sales records, we review them first. CRM exports often carry address gaps, duplicates or inconsistencies — we scope and fix these before analysis starts. No client data? We build the entire picture from ABS Census, GNAF and live competitor sources instead.

Selected work
Problems we've solved

One case study per pillar. Real outputs, real outcomes.

Pillar 01 · Market Expansion

Music school chain — western Melbourne expansion

Scored 8 suburbs across Hobsons Bay and Wyndham to identify the optimal location for a second campus. Analysis combined ABS family demographics, school-age population, income data and competitor mapping. Delivered in 5 business days.

Top suburb identified with zero direct competitors within 4km — decision made in days not months
Pillar 02 · Route-to-Market

QSR franchise — territory rebalance across 42 Victorian sites

Existing franchise territories were built around postcodes rather than population coverage. We redesigned boundaries to equalise catchment sizes, reduce overlap and align with new store openings in Melbourne's growth corridors.

23% reduction in franchisee territory disputes — and three uncovered growth corridors identified
Pillar 03 · Intelligence Systems

Insurance broker — CRM geocoding and automated location reporting

Cleaned, standardised and geocoded 85,000 customer records from a legacy system into CRM with GNAF address matching. Built an automated weekly report showing policy concentration by suburb and emerging coverage gaps.

99.4% address match rate — analysis that had previously been impossible to run
